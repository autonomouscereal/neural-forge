## GAP 3 — RESOLUTION RIDER: the slate's verdicts are written in a currency the instrument cannot mint

**The hole.** This is a decision-machinery gap, not a fold lens, but it is the largest single risk to the round. Count the slate's own effect-size expectations against the 15-prompt instrument: S5 expects 1–2 cases; S6 pre-declares 0 ± 1; S2's kill gate turns on "≤ 3/15 with no per-case mix gain"; S3's sealed motivating delta is exactly +1 (2/15 vs 1/15); ORTHO's rho-census validation leans on a 1-case separation; and wave-20 already ruled one-site reviews below instrument resolution. Five of eight waves will execute kills or escalations on 1-case margins that a 15-prompt frozen review cannot statistically support. L1 is right that only built children + frozen review count — but L1 says nothing about *n*, and the slate's gated escalations (SEGBASIS K=3, VOCAB rung 2, ORTHO hard arm, 29-DIAG rungs, CLIFF resurrection at "≥ 2 cases") are all one noisy review away from firing or dying wrongly.

**Sketch (zero builds, one rider added to §5).** (1) Pre-register a frozen **extension bank** — e.g., 30 additional prompts, drawn by the wave-20 closed-form token rules from the same lanes as the sealed 15, three per capability axis, frozen before any round-2 review runs. (2) Every round-2 child is reviewed on **45 = 15 + 30**, but all gates, escalations, and frontier claims continue to fire **on the sealed 15 only** — full comparability with the 652 rows is preserved; the extension bank orders and annotates, never gates (this is L1-conformant by the same clause that governs the censuses). (3) One binding rule: any KILL or CANCELLATION whose margin on the sealed 15 is exactly 1 case must show extension-bank concordance (delta of the same sign) or it downgrades from "killed" to "unresolved, re-queue" — kills need to be as reliable as claims. (4) Publish per-case mix on all 45 (L5 gets 3x the resolution for free, which is precisely what S5's atlas and the A3 priority vector need).

**Cost:** authoring and freezing the bank before the first review; ~3x review tokens per child; zero GPU, zero solves. Failure to fund this gap does not lose a wave — it silently corrupts several.
