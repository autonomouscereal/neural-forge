## 2. DIAGNOSIS — Why Intelligence Dies Between 0.69x and 0.49x

**The chasm is a budget wall built from four stacked taxes, not one mechanism.** Every child between 0.685x and 0.49x is a ≥2-fold composition graded at direct-assembly quality; nothing in that band exceeds 4/15.

**Tax 1 — additive interference (~3 cases per independently-solved body component).** Each fold is solved against parent activations that the other folds invalidate. Residuals that cancelled get "improved" into residuals that no longer cancel — M1's autopsy sealed this exactly: 5/6 local objectives improved, composed drift 3.6% *worse* (wave 11), and its postmortem named the missing object: the downstream cross term no per-component objective can see. Drift accumulates ~0.042/lane against a ~0.3765 coherence budget — a gap of ~8 lanes that coverage cannot fix (only cancellation can).

**Tax 2 — interface × body multiplication.** L3b (wave 10): 10/15 × 9/15 → 6/15, because the folded interface shifts the input distribution every body solve assumed. Every deep-zone record holder composes a vocab fold (child stream: +15.8% sequence length, 0.1460 segmentation agreement on review prompts) with a CL896 body solved under the *parent* tokenizer. The prescription — "fold the interface first, then re-solve the body against it" — has never been executed. The retokenization death signature ("Earth's Earth-like Earth" word self-substitution, wave 23) is charged as its own failure class.

**Tax 3 — emission collapse before knowledge loss.** Discrimination benchmarks sit within ~2 points of parent while lambada ppl runs 24→99→857 monotonically with depth (waves 8–9); the last surviving behavior is copying ("what was dying last was the copy, not the summary," w28); death is ordered loops → prompt-echo → empty strings (640: empty in 16/16 lanes; 512 fills 8; W704 empty in every lane — NOTES-MX-W/Y, citation corrected). The deep child still *knows* but cannot *emit*: reader-projected generative margins flatten while the content-agnostic copy signal stays sharp. Cyril's "dead, obviously repeating" verdict names this tax precisely.

**Tax 4 — the unprotected ladder.** The review is 89.6% one strict difficulty ladder; the first-dying lanes are always the same (explanation-seasons 0.02, logic-ordering 0.03, coding-dedup 0.05, arithmetic 0.08, multi-turn 0.10 hold rates — L12). Landings are block-constant (10/15 six of six) because no solve defends the ladder top; the summit's extra cases die by default, not necessity (counterproof: V8X13GL01 solves a case the parent fails, at 0.69x).

**What the evidence rules out:** not tensor contention (V16X13, disjoint tensors, same tax); not activation staleness (L17: conditioned re-solve bought exactly +1, once); not ratio per se (K dose-response is non-monotone; wave 25 *withdrew* the input-shift law); not width-death of the surviving content (V20X13W896 answered a lane in full at 0.639x). The chasm is four addressable taxes. The plan attacks each.

## 5. WHAT MAY BE IMPOSSIBLE FOLD-ONLY — AND THE MEASUREMENT THAT DECIDES EACH

Stated as measurements, all inside the fold-only contract. No stage-2 proposal appears here or anywhere in this plan.

1. **The composition-tax constant may be irreducible without gradients.** The record's ceiling for any solve improvement is +3 once, +1 at depth. **Measurement:** #4's two pilots with exact operators, all λ rungs reviewed. A null on both (living pair AND deep solved-writer child) seals: *no closed-form objective, local or global, repairs composition.* Consequence, on the sealed arithmetic: fold-only depth is then bounded by shopping — ~0.60–0.65x for ≥7/15, and parent-band at 0.5x requires every Tier-1/2 mechanism to pay simultaneously without colliding swaps.

2. **Deep-zone degeneracy may live upstream of the terminal read.** **Measurement:** #5's forensic pre-read (free). No margin inversion in the dying children = the terminal surface is exhausted (its +1 at 0.4876x was its ceiling); combined with a #4 null, the entire terminal-objective family is closed at depth.

3. **The ladder top may be unreachable by solver variance.** No λ variation in 28 waves moved arithmetic/logic/coding. **Measurement:** #11 + #6 under the funded rider. Nulls → name the ceiling: fold-only children can hold what their axes' signatures protect, and 14/15 at depth can only come from raising *landings* (base improvements), never from case-shopping.

4. **The MLP mass (47.9%) may be foldable only by deletion.** **Measurement:** #16's probe cell + uniform-ladder rung. Trio ≤ deletion, and ladder rungs dying like wave-14's global cut, means intermediate compression is closed — and then the storage arithmetic is decisive (below).

5. **The instrument may not resolve the chasm at all.** **Measurement:** #1's retro-scoring. A scrambled ordering means the 1–3/15 deep-zone separations — the map this whole plan navigates by — are noise. This is measured before any other GPU is spent.

6. **The 0.25x arithmetic, stated plainly.** Parent 229.7M; 0.25x keeps ~57.4M. The V19 table alone is 19.9M, leaving ~37.5M for a body that is 162.6M in the parent — **4.34x body compression**. Even MLP at 4x everywhere (110M→27.5M) plus attention at 2x (~52M→26M) overshoots the budget; 0.25x requires MLP ≥4x *and* attention ~3x *and* multiple excisions — every sealed killer stacked, at a component count (~10+) where the current ~3-case tax destroys 30+ cases against a 15-case scale. **The deciding measurement is the tax slope:** cases lost per added component on protected bases (retok evidence, KKT-finished, terminal-recalibrated), read directly off Stages 1–4's children. Slope ≤ ~0.5/component → 0.25x is arithmetically in range fold-only and Stage 5 opens. Slope ≥ 2 → 0.25x is out of range fold-only, and that is sealed as a law, not lamented. For 0.5x the same arithmetic is friendly: 0.5x = V19+CL896-class storage exactly (0.483x already built) — the 0.5x question is not *reachability* but *life*, which is what Front A attacks.

7. **What already looks closed (standing bounds, not new measurements):** the ~0.90 reader-energy floor on hidden_size; global simultaneous intermediate cuts at 4x; sub-0.763x lossy interface heads; staleness conditioning. The plan spends nothing on any of them.

**Bottom line for the exit decision:** if #4, #5, #11, and #16 all null, fold-only closes at approximately *shopping-bounded 0.60–0.65x for ≥7/15, with a populated-but-partial chasm on the parent-band column* — and this plan will have produced the sealed proof of that ceiling, which is exactly what a defensible phase-1 exit requires. If even two of them pay, M4 (parent-band at ≤0.55x → ≤0.51x) is the plan's central expectation, and the tax-slope number tells us whether 0.25x is a campaign or a wall.
