## What the numbers imply (arithmetic on the measured rates; tokens/hour = tok/s x 3,600; hours per 100M = 1e8 / tokens per hour)

**0.8B full-parameter distill on one 3090.** At the 4.2 shape (4,096 tokens per sequence, 16 sequences per step) the largest micro-batch that fits is 4, so the step must be mb4 x acc4; the measured mb4/acc1 rate is 4,977 tok/s = 17.9M tokens/hour = 5.6 h per 100M tokens (10M rung 0.56 h, 50M 2.8 h, 200M 11.2 h wall, from `derived_budgets`). At L=1,024 with mb16 it is 5,920 tok/s = 21.3M/h = 4.7 h per 100M. Micro-batch 1 is 2.5-12x slower (2,063 tok/s at L=4,096 = 13.5 h per 100M; 477 tok/s at L=1,024 = 58 h per 100M), so the 0.8B lane must run at the largest fitting micro-batch, not mb1. The plan's "~4k tok/s 0.8B full" assumption was right for the batched shape and 8x optimistic for mb1. Peak is 19.4/21.5 GB reserved of 23.56, leaving no room for an inline 4B teacher on the same card (the 4B forward alone reserved 8.7-9.7 GB); teacher logits must be precomputed or served from another card. The top-k KL term over the same logits costs "the same order or slightly more", so treat these as upper bounds.

**4B on one 3090.** LoRA r=64 on all nine sites: 1,515 tok/s at 4,096 x acc4 (16,384 tokens/step) = 5.45M tokens/hour = 18.3 h per 100M (10M 1.8 h, 50M 9.2 h, 200M 36.7 h); acc1 is 1,087 tok/s = 3.9M/h = 25.5 h per 100M. Peak 12.7/13.3 GB, so a 4B LoRA leaves ~10 GB free on the card. Full-parameter with the hand-rolled CPU-offloaded AdamW is optimizer-bound, not GPU-bound: the host Adam step over 4.2B fp32 parameters takes 75-84 s median regardless of how many tokens were accumulated, so at acc1 the rate is 12.4 tok/s = 44.7k tokens/hour = 2,238 h (93 days) per 100M, and at acc16 it is 147 tok/s = 0.53M/h = 189 h (7.9 days) per 100M (10M 18.9 h, 50M 94.5 h, 200M 378 h). Derived, unmeasured: the fwd+bwd share of the acc16 step is roughly 20 s for 16,384 tokens (~820 tok/s ceiling), so even acc64 would only reach roughly 380 tok/s (~70 h per 100M) unless the host step is made ~10x faster (it is a plain Python loop over 44 threads on a 54.8 GB state). For the <=4B stage-D ladder at the 100M-200M rungs, LoRA is 10x cheaper than CPU-offloaded full-param on this box; full-param 4B is only viable at the 10M rung or after the host optimizer is rewritten (the D-R5 "4B CPU-offload full-param vs LoRA" line).

**Teacher side, for context.** The streamed 27B teacher on one 3090 gives 696 tok/s forward-only at mb8 (2.5M tokens/hour, 40 h per 100M tokens of top-k logits, 80 h for the 200M rung) and 171 tok/s at mb1; each pass re-reads ~45.7 GB of safetensors, so it is read-bound and must be precomputed, never run on-the-fly beside the student. The 27B LoRA split (RTX 8000 + 3090) measured 151 tok/s fwd+bwd at L=2,048 = 0.54M tokens/hour = 184 h per 100M (734.7 GPU-hours for 200M), above the D-R5-1 floor of 100 tok/s but with 6 of 13 steps skipped by the fp16 loss scaler, so the effective update rate is lower, and the 4,096 shape was never measured.
