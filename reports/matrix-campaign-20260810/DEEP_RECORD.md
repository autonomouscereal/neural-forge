## 8. DEEP FAMILY LAWS — every regularity this band has, and what breaks it

**Added at wave 37.** Each law below is stated with the rows it is fitted on, the
rows that break it, and what would falsify it. Two of them are corrections to
laws earlier notes stated wrongly, and those corrections are the first thing in
each entry rather than a footnote.

### 8.1 The terminal-gain arm lands at THREE, and the delta is not the invariant

**The law as earlier notes stated it is wrong at one of its six entries.**
`NOTES-MX-AM` and `NOTES-MX-AN` both say the λ0.3 arm on a `CL896` vocabulary
rung is worth *exactly +1* at K = 10,240, 14,336, 16,384, 19,456 and 20,480, and
+2 only at K = 12,288. **At K = 16,384 it is +2**: `ARCH.V16CL896` seals ONE
(wave 23) and `ARCH.V16CL896G03` seals THREE (wave 34), there is no superseding
row for either, and **wave 34's own seal note says so in words** — *"the gain
buys TWO here as it bought two on the K=12,288 base."* The law was written a wave
later and contradicted the seal it was summarising.

| K | control | λ0.3 | Δ | λ1.0 | Δ |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 20,480 `V20CL896` | 2 | **3** | +1 | — | — |
| 19,456 `V19CL896` | 2 | **3** | +1 | — | — |
| 16,384 `V16CL896` | **1** | **3** | **+2** | **2** | **+1** |
| 14,336 `V14CL896` | 2 | **3** | +1 | **2** | **+0** |
| 12,288 `V12CL896` | 2 | **4** | +2 | 2 | +0 |
| 10,240 `V10CL896` | 2 | 3 | +1 | 3 | +1 |
| 9,216 `V9CL896` | 2 | 3 | +1 | **4** | **+2** |

**Restated: the λ0.3 arm lands the child at THREE at five of the six K values,
regardless of where its control was.** The invariant is the arm's COUNT, not its
delta. `ARCH.V12CL896G03`'s four is the single exception in seven ladders.

**What falsifies it:** a λ0.3 arm on a `CL896` rung that reads anything other
than three or four. K = 8,192 is the only untried rung and its control is the
campaign's 0/15 vocabulary death, so that is the test.

### 8.2 λ1.0 is real, is not monotone in K, and does not track stored ratio

Wave 36 recorded λ1.0's first win in twelve ladders and asked whether it was
K-specific. Wave 37 completed the ladder and the answer is **neither monotone nor
ratio-driven**: +1 at K=16,384, **+0 at K=14,336**, +0 at K=12,288, +1 at
K=10,240, +2 at K=9,216. The K=12,288 zero is not the shallow end of a trend; it
is one of two zeros with gains on both sides of them.

And it is **not** a depth effect. `ARCH.V12CL896X09G10` runs λ1.0 at K=12,288 on
an excised body at **0.420779x — deeper in stored ratio than the K=9,216 rung
where λ1.0 bought two** — and it buys **zero**. So whatever λ1.0 is doing is a
property of the vocabulary rung and the body, not of how small the child is.

### 8.3 The λ objective anti-correlates with its own solver criterion — n = 13 ladders

The terminal-gain solver minimises held-out logit relative SSE. Across thirteen
ladders the arm with the **better** objective has read **worse** on the frozen
review eight times, better once, and tied four times.

| ladder | better objective | better review |
|---|---|---|
| `V9CL896` (wave 36) | λ0.3 (0.1769 vs 0.1785) | **λ1.0** (4 vs 3) |
| `V14CL896` (wave 37) | λ0.3 (0.1993 vs 0.2013) | **λ0.3** (3 vs 2) — the ONLY agreement |
| `V12CL896X09` (wave 37) | λ0.3 (0.2470 vs 0.2516) | **λ1.0** (2 vs 1) |
| `V12CL896X13` (wave 36) | λ0.3, 23% improvement, the largest recorded | **tie at 1**, by a lane swap |
| nine earlier ladders (waves 24–35) | — | five inversions, four ties |

**Wave 37 is the first wave in the campaign where the solver criterion and the
review agreed on a λ ladder, and it happened on the ladder where λ1.0 gained
nothing.** The agreement is at K = 14,336; one K value away in either direction
the ordering inverts again.

**What this law is for:** it is the reason the ladder runs as a ladder. The
objective is not permitted to choose the arm, because on this family it is a
coin-flip that lands tails eight times in thirteen.
